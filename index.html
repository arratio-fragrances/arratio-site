Introducing ARRATIO

Two distinct compositions created through careful formulation, evaluation, and refinement. The first ARRATIO fragrances are currently preparing for release.

Ascend

Woody Aromatic

A modern woody-citrus scent that evolves with a sense of forward momentum.

Ascend moves seamlessly from a bright citrus opening into aromatic spice, fresh florals, and finally musky woods. Bergamot and blackcurrant bring an immediate wave of freshness at the top, while pink peppercorn adds spicy energy through the heart. Patchouli, oakmoss, ambergris, and soft white musk emerge gradually in the drydown, bringing depth to the fragrance as it evolves while maintaining the freshness established from the first spray.

Key Notes Bergamot · Pink Peppercorn · Blackcurrant · Patchouli · Oakmoss · Ambergris

Radiance

Floral Woody Musk

A luminous floral where fresh greens and airy petals settle into clean musk and soft woods.

Radiance opens with fresh green notes before unfolding into its true heart; an airy bouquet of jasmine, muguet, violet, and delicate florals. Subtle fruit adds brightness through the heart, while cedarwood and clean white musks create a smooth, grounded finish. Light, diffusive, and fresh, Radiance is designed around the clarity and openness of spring and summer.

Key Notes Green Leaves · Jasmine · Muguet · Violet · White Musk · Cedarwood

About

ARRATIO

ARRATIO is an independent fragrance project built around the process of composition. Each fragrance begins as an idea and develops through repeated formulation, evaluation, aging, and refinement.

Fragrances are produced in small batches using professional perfumery materials sourced from established industry suppliers, with attention given to balance, performance, and the way each composition develops throughout its wear.
